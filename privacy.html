Privacy Policy – Cameron Craig Photography Introduction At Cameron Craig Photography, your privacy matters. This page explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website. Information We Collect Analytics data: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our site. This includes anonymous information such as pages visited, time spent, and general location (city/country). Cookies: Small text files stored in your browser that help us track analytics and improve your experience. How We Use Information To measure site performance and improve content. To understand which pages and images are most engaging. To ensure the website runs smoothly across devices. Your Choices Opt‑out of cookies: You can decline cookies via the banner when you first visit. Browser settings: You can clear or block cookies in your browser at any time. Do Not Track: If your browser sends a “Do Not Track” signal, we respect it. Data Sharing We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information. Analytics data is processed by Google in accordance with their Privacy Policy. Contact For questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page.
